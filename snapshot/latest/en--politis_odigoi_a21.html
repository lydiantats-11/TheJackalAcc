A21 — Child benefit

A monthly benefit per child, paid in six bimonthly instalments, based on the latest assessed tax return. The amount depends on the income category.

What it provides

1st–2nd child: €70 / €42 / €28 a month · 3rd+: €140 / €84 / €56 a month

Requirements

  • At least one dependent child
  • Living in Greece (overseas Greeks and foreign nationals need a valid residence permit)
  • Income category: limits are not stated on the page checked

Documents

The list of documents has not been recorded yet. The list on the authority's official page applies.

Step by step

  1. Apply online at opeka.gr or idika.gr with TAXISnet credentials.
  2. An application counts only once finally submitted and approved.

Deadlines

New or amended applications until Fri 13 Nov 2026, 18:00 Athens time (platform reopened 5 Oct 2026)
