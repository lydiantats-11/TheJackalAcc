Article 5B — 7% tax for pensioners with a foreign pension

Άρθρο 5Β ΚΦΕ — 7% φόρος για συνταξιούχους με σύνταξη εξωτερικού

Summary

A flat 7% rate on all foreign-sourced income, for 15 tax years in total.

Summary written by the platform team — not official text. English text is the platform's translation of the Greek official sources, not an official translation.

Key facts

Amount / benefit
7% tax for 15 years
Application period
Depends on the year you moved your residence — see the table below.
As the source puts it: «Apply by 31 October of the year of arrival or of the following tax year at the latest; documents by 30 November of the year of the application. Moved by 2 Jul: apply that year or the next; moved after 2 Jul: apply for the next year (Decision A.1192/2026, Article 4(1)).»

Periods marked «not confirmed» follow from the 2026 rule, but no official act has confirmed them for that year yet.

Disagreements between official sources
  • First year of the 15 years: Decision A.1192/2026 (Article 4(3)) says «for the tax year for which the application is filed and for fifteen (15) tax years in total»; Article 5B(4) of the Income Tax Code, as republished (taxheaven), says «starts from the next tax year for which the application is filed». We have not confirmed which year is the first; check it in ΑΑΔΕ's approval decision.

How to apply

How to apply (from the official source)
  1. Apply through myAADE («My requests») or to the KE.FO.D.E. of Attica or Thessaloniki (in person, by post or courier), by 31 October of the year the residence moves or of the following year.
  2. Supporting documents may be completed by 30 November of the year of the application.
  3. ΑΑΔΕ reviews the application within 60 days of submission (Decision A.1192/2026).

Applications are made on the authority's official page. Check the documents and deadline there before you submit.
