How it works

From the official source to an answer you can understand.

More about the method
  1. 1

    Official sources

    We gather programmes from official Greek and European sources.

  2. 2

    Checking

    We record deadlines, amounts and requirements with a link to the source and the date checked.

  3. 3

    Matching

    We compare the recorded requirements with your answers, using fixed rules.

  4. 4

    Clear result

    You see what appears to fit, what is missing and where to apply.

A21 — Child benefit

A monthly benefit per child, paid in six bimonthly instalments, based on the latest assessed tax return. The amount depends on the income category.

Regular unemployment benefit

A benefit for people who lost salaried work, lasting 5 to 12 months depending on insured days. A 10% increase for dependants, subject to conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about what the platform does and does not do.

All questions
What is Jackal Accountant?

A platform that gathers grants, benefits and programmes from official Greek and European sources and helps you find the ones that apply to you. It is not an accounting firm.

Is it a government service?

No. It is an independent information platform. Applications are always made on the authorities' official websites.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse opportunities. You only need an account for your personal profile and saved opportunities.

If I see "Potentially eligible", will I be approved?

Not necessarily. It means the requirements we have recorded appear to be met by your answers. The responsible authority makes the final decision.

How recent is the information?

Every listing shows the date its official source was checked. Automatic updates are not switched on yet, so always check the official page before applying.
