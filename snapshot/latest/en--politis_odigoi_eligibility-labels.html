What the eligibility labels mean

How to read the results in "For Me", and why they are never a final answer.

General explanation1 min read
A general explanation by the platform; it does not describe the terms of a specific programme.

The labels

  • "Potentially eligible": every requirement we have recorded appears to be met by your answers.
  • "Potentially — needs confirmation": it appears to fit, but the authority will check some details with documents.
  • "More information needed": an answer is missing or the requirements are not fully recorded.
  • "Probably not eligible": a requirement does not appear to be met.

Good to know

  • "Not sure" and "Prefer not to answer" never count against you.
  • Categories, interests and industries only help you find opportunities; they never change eligibility.
  • The responsible authority always makes the final decision.

This guide explains the official information in plain words. It does not guarantee eligibility, funding or approval; the responsible authority always makes the final decision.
