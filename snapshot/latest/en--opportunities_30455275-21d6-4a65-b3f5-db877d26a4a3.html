Student housing allowance

Official facts

Annual allowance for students who study away from home. Applications for 2025–26 have closed.

Summary written by the platform team — not official text. English text is the platform's translation of the Greek official sources, not an official translation.

Key facts

Amount / benefit
Per the Ministry (29 Jul 2026): €1,500, or €2,000 if sharing; €2,000 or €2,500 outside Attica/Thessaloniki. Not stated explicitly for 2025–26.
The source does not state every part of the amount.
Application period
30 Jun 2026 → 31 Jul 2026
As the source puts it: «2025–26: 30 Jun–31 Jul 2026 and 17–22 Sep 2026 · 2026–27: not announced»

  • Valid academic ID
  • Greek or EU citizen
  • Tax resident in Greece, with a tax number and a filed tax return

How to apply

How to apply (from the official source)

Applications are made on the authority's official page. Check the documents and deadline there before you submit.
