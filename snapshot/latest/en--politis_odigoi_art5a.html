Article 5A — alternative taxation for investors

A flat €100,000 a year on all foreign-sourced income, for 15 tax years, with an investment of at least €500,000 in Greece.

What it provides

€100,000/year regardless of foreign income, 15 years

Requirements

  • Not a Greek tax resident in 7 of the 8 previous years
  • Investment of at least €500,000 (property, businesses, securities) within 3 years

Documents

The list of documents has not been recorded yet. The list on the authority's official page applies.

Step by step

  1. Apply through myAADE («My requests») or to the KE.FO.D.E. of Attica or Thessaloniki (in person, by post or courier), by 30 September of the year the residence moves or of the following year.
  2. Supporting documents may be completed by 31 October of the year of the application.
  3. ΑΑΔΕ decides within 60 days of submission and no later than the last working day of November (Decision A.1147/2026).

Deadlines

Apply by 30 September of the year of arrival or of the following tax year at the latest; documents by 31 October of the year of the application. Moved by 2 Jul: apply that year or the next; moved after 2 Jul: apply for the next year (Decision A.1147/2026, Article 4(1)).
