Article 5B — 7% tax for pensioners with a foreign pension

ΑΑΔΕ (tax authority) · Checked on 14 October 2026

Plain explanations of programmes and applications, from the official pages. Always check the official source before applying.

What it is

A flat 7% rate on all foreign-sourced income, for 15 tax years from the year after the application.

Who can apply

  • Moving tax residence to Greece
  • Not a Greek tax resident in 5 of the 6 previous years
  • From a country with an administrative tax cooperation agreement
  • Receives foreign-source pension income

How to apply

  1. Apply to ΑΑΔΕ by 31 March of the tax year.

What you'll need

The full list of documents is set on the authority's official page.

Official sources

  • ΑΑΔΕ — Φορολογικά κίνητρα άρθρων 5Α, 5Β, 5Γ (PDF)
Deadline
By 31 March of the tax year
Amount / benefit
7% tax for 15 years
