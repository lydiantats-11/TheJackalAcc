Start a Business (Xekino Epicheirimatika)

Competitiveness 2021–2027 (ESPA), ESF+ · EFEPAE (intermediate body) · Official page ↗

FinancialExpensesΚΑΔDocumentsProcedureTimeline

Key conditions

  • Minimum operation: two verified six-month periods, otherwise withdrawal and repayment (FAQ Q35)
  • 12 or 18 months of continuous operation from the AADE start date
  • 100% public funding; de minimis, ceiling €300,000 per single undertaking over 3 years (Reg. 2023/2831)
  • Individual income in the last tax year up to €30,000 (Table Γ of the tax assessment)
  • Budget €70m (€55m less developed regions, €15m Attica & South Aegean), with up to +30% (2nd amendment)

Eligible expenses

  • Operating costs (rent, electricity, telephone, water, heating, shared costs, incubator hosting)
  • Third-party fees (legal, accounting, advisory, business plan)
  • Promotion, networking, trade fairs
  • Consumables
  • Equipment: purchase, leasing or depreciation
  • The beneficiary's own social security contributions
  • Payroll of a new job (unit cost)

Simplified costs: no paid invoices are submitted (FAQ Q37, call definitions). Source ↗

Eligible activity codes (ΚΑΔ)

The business must operate only under eligible codes in Annex II (main or secondary), from its start with AADE until payment.

The full list (Annex II) has not been extracted yet; see the 1st amendment.

Sectors named on the body's page: ICT, STEM, tourism

Activating a primary-sector code during the project makes the business ineligible (FAQ Q18).

Application and payment procedure

  1. 1Check that your activity code is in Annex II and that you meet the conditions (degree after 1/1/2016, income, premises).
  2. 2Prepare the business plan, which is the content of the application.
  3. 3Submit online in OPSKE (app.opske.gr) between 06/05/2026 15:00 and 30/06/2026 15:00; applications outside OPSKE are not accepted.
  4. 4Comparative evaluation of all applications and publication of results.
  5. 5Start-up (category A) within 365 days of approval; operation verified every six months.
  6. 6Payment per verified milestone (Annex VII), without checking invoices.
