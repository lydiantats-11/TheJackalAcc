Summary

A flat €100,000 a year on all foreign-sourced income, for 15 tax years, with an investment of at least €500,000 in Greece.

Summary written by the platform team — not official text. English text is the platform's translation of the Greek official sources, not an official translation.

Who can apply — summary of conditions
  • Not a Greek tax resident in 7 of the 8 previous years
  • Investment of at least €500,000 (property, businesses, securities) within 3 years
