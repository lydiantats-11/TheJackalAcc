Regular unemployment benefit

A benefit for people who lost salaried work, lasting 5 to 12 months depending on insured days. A 10% increase for dependants, subject to conditions.

What it provides

Duration 5–12 months · amount: not stated on the page

Requirements

  • Unemployed on the day of the application
  • Salaried job ended or terminated by the employer, with contributions to the unemployment branch
  • Unemployment card issued
  • Required insured days in critical periods: the number is not stated

Documents

The list of documents has not been recorded yet. The list on the authority's official page applies.

Step by step

  1. Apply on gov.gr or ΔΥΠΑ e-services with TAXISnet credentials, or at a ΚΠΑ2 office.
  2. Form Ε6 (termination) or Ε7 (fixed-term end), IBAN.

What we don't know yet

  • Since 2025 a new unemployment benefit is piloted for randomly selected claimants, with a different amount and duration (up to 16 months per the announcement, up to 24 per the FAQ). The application is the same.
  • Some details (e.g. amounts or limits) are not stated on the official pages we checked.
