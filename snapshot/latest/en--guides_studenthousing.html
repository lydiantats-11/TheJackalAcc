Student housing allowance

Ministry of Education · Checked on 14 October 2026

Plain explanations of programmes and applications, from the official pages. Always check the official source before applying.

What it is

Annual allowance for students who study away from home. Applications for 2025–26 have closed.

Who can apply

  • Valid academic ID
  • Greek or EU citizen
  • Tax resident in Greece, with a tax number and a filed tax return
  • Income and other criteria: in the circular, not checked

How to apply

  1. Apply at stegastiko.minedu.gov.gr with TAXISnet credentials when the next period opens.

What you'll need

The full list of documents is set on the authority's official page.

Official sources

  • minedu.gov.gr — ανακοίνωση 17/9/2026
  • Portal — Προθεσμίες & προϋποθέσεις
  • minedu.gov.gr — ανακοίνωση 29/7/2026
Deadline
2025–26: 30 Jun–31 Jul 2026 and 17–22 Sep 2026 · 2026–27: not announced
Amount / benefit
Per the Ministry (29 Jul 2026): €1,500, or €2,000 if sharing; €2,000 or €2,500 outside Attica/Thessaloniki. Not stated explicitly for 2025–26.
