How to apply on an official platform

The general steps before and after you submit. Each programme's official page sets its own rules.

Before applying

  • Open the official page from the listing and check the deadline, requirements and documents.
  • Many Greek online services ask you to log in with TAXISnet credentials; each service's page says so.
  • Prepare the documents the official page asks for, not lists from other sources.

After submitting

  • Make sure the application was finally submitted and keep its number or receipt.
  • Follow results and announcements on the authority's official page.

Please note

  • Jackal Accountant never submits applications and never asks for passwords or payment.
  • If a page that doesn't belong to a public authority asks for passwords or money for an application, don't proceed.
