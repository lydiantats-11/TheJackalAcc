"Spiti mou II" — first-home loans

First-home loans for ages 25–50, up to €190,000 (up to 90% of the value, property up to €250,000). Applications closed on 31 May 2026 and contracts on 31 Aug 2026; 15,097 loans were approved and 14,114 contracts signed. No new application period is announced.
