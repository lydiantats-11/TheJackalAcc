DiscoverEU — free travel pass for 18-year-olds

About 40,000 travel passes, mainly for trains, with a discount card and support for travellers with a disability. Travel between 1 Mar 2027 and 31 May 2028, alone or with up to 4 friends.

What it provides

Travel pass + discount card (not a cash amount)

Requirements

  • Born between 1 Jan 2008 and 31 Dec 2008
  • Citizen or resident of an EU state or Iceland, Liechtenstein, North Macedonia, Norway, Serbia, Türkiye

Documents

The list of documents has not been recorded yet. The list on the authority's official page applies.

Step by step

  1. Apply on the DiscoverEU page (an "Apply now" button appears while the round is open).
  2. You need an ID, passport or residence-permit number and must complete a quiz.

Deadlines

15 Oct 2026, 12:00 Brussels time

What we don't know yet

  • Travel length: the main page says "up to 7 days within one month", the 28 Sep 2026 notice says "1 to 30 days".
