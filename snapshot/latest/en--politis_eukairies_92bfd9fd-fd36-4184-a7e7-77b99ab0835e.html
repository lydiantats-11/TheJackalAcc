Article 5C — 50% income-tax exemption for people moving tax residence to Greece

Άρθρο 5Γ ΚΦΕ — 50% απαλλαγή φόρου για όσους μεταφέρουν τη φορολογική κατοικία τους στην Ελλάδα

ΑΑΔΕ (tax authority)

What you get50% exemption for 7 tax years
Who appliesCitizens
TimeRolling — no closing date
What to do nowRead the guide, then apply on the official pageGuideOfficial application page

Summary

Income tax is exempted on 50% of income from employment or business activity in Greece, for 7 tax years. It is a tax incentive, not a grant.

Summary written by the platform team — not official text. English text is the platform's translation of the Greek official sources, not an official translation.

Key facts

Amount / benefit
50% exemption for 7 tax years
Application period
No fixed deadline
As the source puts it: «Work starts by 2 Jul: apply by the end of that year · after 2 Jul: by the end of the next year»

Who can apply — summary of conditions
  • Moving tax residence to Greece
    Official wording
    «μεταφέρει τη φορολογική του κατοικία»
  • Not a Greek tax resident in 5 of the 6 previous years
    Official wording
    «δεν ήταν φορολογικός κάτοικος της Ελλάδος τα προηγούμενα πέντε (5) από τα έξι (6) έτη»
  • From an EU/EEA state or a country with an administrative cooperation agreement
    Official wording
    «μεταφέρει τη φορολογική του κατοικία από κράτος μέλος της Ε.Ε. ή του Ε.Ο.Χ.»
  • Employment with a Greek entity/permanent establishment, or a sole proprietorship in Greece
    Official wording
    «παρέχει υπηρεσίες στην Ελλάδα στο πλαίσιο εργασιακής σχέσης … δήλωση έναρξης εργασιών ατομικής επιχείρησης»
  • Declares intention to stay in Greece for at least 2 years

How to apply

How to apply (from the official source)
  1. Apply online through myAADE with TAXISnet credentials.
  2. See ΑΑΔΕ's FAQ and user guide.

Applications are made on the authority's official page. Check the documents and deadline there before you submit.
