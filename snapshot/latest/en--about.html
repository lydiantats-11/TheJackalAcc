About Jackal Accountant

We make public funding understandable: which opportunities exist, who they are for and exactly what the official sources require.

Mission & visionPrinciplesHow it worksLabelsSourcesQuestions
Our mission

To help people, families and businesses find the public opportunities that apply to them, understand the official requirements and follow the correct application process, without promises.

Our vision

A European knowledge infrastructure for public funding, where every programme comes from an official source, every requirement is documented and every result can be checked.

Our principles

Every change to the platform is judged against them.

Official sources only

Every deadline, amount and requirement links to the authority's official page and the date it was checked. Anything not stated is shown as "not stated".

Evidence behind every result

Checks are made rule by rule, and you can see where each result came from.

No promises

We say "potentially eligible", never "approved". The responsible authority always makes the final decision.

Only what is needed

We only ask what a requirement needs. We never ask for tax numbers, passwords or documents to search.

Measured, not claimed

Accuracy and coverage are measured with repeatable checks before any figure is published.

What we do

We gather opportunities from official Greek and European sources, organise them into simple categories and show you which appear to apply to you, based on your profile.

What we don't do

  • We are not a government service or an accounting firm.
  • We don't apply on your behalf.
  • We don't guarantee eligibility, approval or funding.
  • We don't give tax or legal advice.

How it works

From the official announcement to the result you see, in four steps.

  1. 1

    We collect from official sources

    We read official pages and, where available, their official data, such as the EU Funding & Tenders Portal.

  2. 2

    We record the requirements

    Each requirement is written as a clear rule, with the official wording and a link to the source. Anything uncertain is marked as uncertain.

  3. 3

    We check against your profile

    Your answers are compared with each rule separately. Categories, interests and industries only help you find opportunities.

  4. 4

    We follow changes

    We record extensions, amendments and disagreements between sources, and every listing shows its history.

Frequently asked questions

What is Jackal Accountant?

A platform that gathers grants, benefits and programmes from official Greek and European sources and helps you find the ones that apply to you. It is not an accounting firm.

Is it a government service?

No. It is an independent information platform. Applications are always made on the authorities' official websites.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse opportunities. You only need an account for your personal profile and saved opportunities.

If I see "Potentially eligible", will I be approved?

Not necessarily. It means the requirements we have recorded appear to be met by your answers. The responsible authority makes the final decision.

How recent is the information?

Every listing shows the date its official source was checked. Automatic updates are not switched on yet, so always check the official page before applying.

What happens to my data?

We never ask for tax or social-security numbers or documents. Every question accepts "Not sure" or "Prefer not to answer". You can download or delete your data at any time.
