DiscoverEU — free travel pass for 18-year-olds

About 40,000 travel passes, mainly for trains, with a discount card and support for travellers with a disability. Travel between 1 Mar 2027 and 31 May 2028, alone or with up to 4 friends.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Rolling

EIC Accelerator — funding for innovative start-ups and SMEs

Grant for TRL 6–8 innovation activities over 24 months, and/or equity investment. Full applications are batched on set dates.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Rolling

Article 5C — 50% income-tax exemption for people moving tax residence to Greece

Income tax is exempted on 50% of income from employment or business activity in Greece, for 7 tax years. It is a tax incentive, not a grant.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Rolling

Article 5B — 7% tax for pensioners with a foreign pension

A flat 7% rate on all foreign-sourced income, for 15 tax years from the year after the application.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Rolling

Article 5A — alternative taxation for investors

A flat €100,000 a year on all foreign-sourced income, for 15 tax years, with an investment of at least €500,000 in Greece.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Closed

"Spiti mou II" — first-home loans

First-home loans for ages 25–50, up to €190,000 (up to 90% of the value, property up to €250,000). Applications closed on 31 May 2026 and contracts on 31 Aug 2026; 15,097 loans were approved and 14,114 contracts signed. No new application period is announced.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Closed

Student housing allowance

Annual allowance for students who study away from home. Applications for 2025–26 have closed.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Open

A21 — Child benefit

A monthly benefit per child, paid in six bimonthly instalments, based on the latest assessed tax return. The amount depends on the income category.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
Rolling

Regular unemployment benefit

A benefit for people who lost salaried work, lasting 5 to 12 months depending on insured days. A 10% increase for dependants, subject to conditions.

Checked on 14 Oct 2026
