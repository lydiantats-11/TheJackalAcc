How to apply for the regular unemployment benefit (ΔΥΠΑ)

A guide to a new application for the regular unemployment benefit, from getting the unemployment card to the decision and the attendance declarations, based on the official pages of ΔΥΠΑ, gov.gr and Mitos.

Who it is for

  • Employees whose employment ended or was terminated by the employer and who paid contributions to ΔΥΠΑ's unemployment branch.
  • First-time applicants. People who received the benefit before have extra conditions on the official page.

What it provides

A monthly benefit for 5 to 12 months, depending on insured days. The amount is calculated on the minimum wage, so it changes when that changes. ΔΥΠΑ's Greek page (undated) currently gives €565.00 a month for average monthly pay of €493.09 or more, €423.75 for €246.55–€493.08 and €282.50 up to €246.54, plus 10% for each dependent family member. The amount that applies to you is set in ΔΥΠΑ's decision.

Requirements

  • Your salaried employment ended or was terminated by the employer.
  • On the day you apply you do not work for another employer or as self-employed, and you are able and available to work.
  • Case one: at least 125 days of work (100 for construction workers) in the 14 months before the end of work, not counting the last two months.
  • Case two: at least 200 days of work (180 for construction workers) in the two years before the end of work.
  • The page also mentions 80 days of salaried work in each of the two previous years; confirm with ΔΥΠΑ which case this applies to.
  • Registration as unemployed with a valid unemployment card.

Documents

  • Termination of employment (Form Ε6) or end of fixed-term contract (Form Ε7). According to Mitos it is retrieved automatically.
  • Indirect insurance cover of dependent family members, if any (for the 10% increase).
  • IBAN of an account in your name, only if you have not declared it before or it changed.
  • Insured days are retrieved automatically (Mitos).

Step by step

  1. Register as unemployed and get an unemployment card on ΔΥΠΑ's e-services with your TAXISnet credentials.
    • Registration is online; you do not need to visit an office.
    • Under «Υποβολή ηλεκτρονικής αίτησης εγγραφής» (online registration) you choose the ΚΠΑ2 office for where you live.
    Confirmed step
  2. Apply within 60 days from the day after your employment ended.
    Confirmed step
  3. Log in to ΔΥΠΑ's e-services or to the gov.gr service «Αίτηση επιδόματος ανεργίας» (unemployment benefit application) with your TAXISnet credentials.
    Confirmed step
  4. Choose «ΥΠΟΒΟΛΗ ΑΙΤΗΣΗΣ» (submit application) and then «ΑΙΤΗΣΗ ΤΑΚΤΙΚΗΣ ΕΠΙΔΟΤΗΣΗΣ ΑΝΕΡΓΙΑΣ» (regular unemployment benefit application).
    Confirmed step
  5. Declare any dependent family members, your payment account and anything else requested. While submitting you are told the conditions and your obligations and sign a solemn declaration under Law 1599/1986.
    Confirmed step
  6. Check your details before final submission.
    Screen still to be confirmed on the official service
  7. Keep the receipt or the application number.
    Screen still to be confirmed on the official service
  8. Follow your application's progress on ΔΥΠΑ's e-services.
    Screen still to be confirmed on the official service

Deadlines

Apply within 60 days from the day after your employment ended. The unemployment card is valid for three months and can be renewed from 20 days before it expires. Processing time according to Mitos: 3 months.

After you apply

  • The office checks your details. If something is missing you have 20 days to provide it; otherwise a rejection may follow after 3 months.
  • An approval sets the duration, the amount and your attendance months. Against a rejection you can file an administrative appeal (Mitos).
  • Make an attendance declaration in the third month of each quarter. If it is missed for two consecutive attendance months, the benefit is suspended.
  • The benefit is paid into your account monthly.

Common mistakes and warnings

  • The 60 days count from the day after your job ended; don't leave it to the last moment.
  • If you don't renew the unemployment card in time, it is deleted automatically.
  • If you find work or something changes, tell ΔΥΠΑ within 8 working days. Amounts paid when you were not entitled must be repaid.
  • The payment account must be in your name.
  • Jackal Accountant never submits applications and never asks for TAXISnet credentials.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply if I resigned?

The official page refers to employment that ended or was terminated by the employer. For your own case, ask ΔΥΠΑ's ΚΠΑ2 office.

How long is the benefit paid?

125–149 days: 5 months; 150–179: 6; 180–219: 8; 220–249: 10; 250 or more: 12 months (ΔΥΠΑ table 1.A, 14-month period). Other tables apply to the two-year period and to construction workers.

What is the new pilot unemployment benefit?

Since 28/3/2025 ΔΥΠΑ has piloted a new benefit for about 15,000 randomly selected claimants who had no unemployment benefit in the previous 4 years. It lasts up to 24 months and is calculated differently. You do not choose it; the application is the same. Its terms are not covered in this guide.

Can I apply in person?

Applying online is the main way. Mitos also lists applying in person at a ΚΠΑ2 office and says the online-only rule applied under the COVID-19 measures. If you need help, contact your ΚΠΑ2 office first.

What we don't know yet

  • Amounts: ΔΥΠΑ's Greek page gives €565.00 (top band), the English page €479.00. Neither is dated. The Greek page's daily amount (€22.60) equals 55% of the €41.09 daily wage in force from 1/4/2026, Both pages describe the same scheme with the same three bands; every English figure matches the €34.84 daily wage in force from 1/4/2023 (according to a news report, not an official source). So the English page most likely shows 2023 amounts. This is our own calculation; only ΔΥΠΑ can confirm the amount.
  • How to apply: ΔΥΠΑ's Greek page says «only» online but also in person at a ΚΠΑ2 office. Mitos (updated 14/10/2026) explains the online-only rule belonged to the COVID-19 measures. We found no official notice on whether every ΚΠΑ2 office accepts in-person applications today.
  • Pilot new benefit: the Ministry's announcement (23/12/2024, before launch) said up to 16 months; ΔΥΠΑ's launch notice (28/3/2025) says up to 24 months. This guide follows the later notice of the body that runs it.
  • The screens after choosing the application have not been confirmed with screenshots; those steps are marked.
