Greece

12 Oct 2026AADE (Independent Authority for Public Revenue)

Up to 72 instalments, legal entities included

Applies to individuals and legal entities, for debts overdue by 31/12/2023 (tax office, KEMEF, KEVEIS, customs). Debts under an arrangement on 21/04/2026 and recovery of EU state aid are excluded. Condition: income tax returns for the last 5 years filed. Minimum instalment €30.

DebtsDeadline: 31 Dec 2026

Official text: Εγκύκλιος Ε.2060/12-10-2026 (άρθρο 19 ν.5313/2026· Α.1141/2026) · Checked against the official text · Checked 20 Oct 2026

7 Oct 2026AADE (Independent Authority for Public Revenue)

New competent body for debts from loans guaranteed by the Hellenic Development Bank

The head of the tax office (or of the collection unit, where jointly competent) becomes responsible. Eligibility, instalments and deadlines do not change.
