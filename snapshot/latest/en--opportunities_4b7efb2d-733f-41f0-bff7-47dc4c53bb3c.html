A21 — Child benefit

Α21 — Επίδομα παιδιού

OPEKA

OverviewKey factsEligibilityHow to applySourcesChange history
Official facts

A monthly benefit per child, paid in six bimonthly instalments, based on the latest assessed tax return. The amount depends on the income category.

Summary written by the platform team — not official text. English text is the platform's translation of the Greek official sources, not an official translation.

Who can apply (official conditions)
  • At least one dependent child
  • Living in Greece (overseas Greeks and foreign nationals need a valid residence permit)
  • Income category: limits are not stated on the page checked

How to apply

How to apply (from the official source)
  1. Apply online at opeka.gr or idika.gr with TAXISnet credentials.
  2. An application counts only once finally submitted and approved.

Applications are made on the authority's official page. Check the documents and deadline there before you submit.
