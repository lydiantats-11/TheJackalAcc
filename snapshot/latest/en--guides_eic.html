EIC Accelerator — funding for innovative start-ups and SMEs

European Innovation Council (EIC) · Checked on 14 October 2026

Plain explanations of programmes and applications, from the official pages. Always check the official source before applying.

What it is

Grant for TRL 6–8 innovation activities over 24 months, and/or equity investment. Full applications are batched on set dates.

Who can apply

  • Start-up or SME, a person intending to launch an SME, or a small mid-cap (equity only)
  • Small mid-caps: up to 499 employees

How to apply

  1. Short application, then full application on the EIC platform.
  2. Full applications are batched on 7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep and 4 Nov 2026.

What you'll need

The full list of documents is set on the authority's official page.
