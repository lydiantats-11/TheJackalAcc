# Where the three tax-regime guides belong (recommendation, 16 Oct 2026 — not implemented)

The guides: Άρθρο 5Γ (people moving tax residence to work in Greece), 5Β (pensioners with a foreign pension),
5Α (investors). What they share: each is an **alternative way of being taxed** for people who move their tax residence
to Greece. None is a benefit payment, a grant, a job programme or a business subsidy.

| Option | What changes | Fit for 5Γ / 5Β / 5Α | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Keep the eight categories as approved, put all three in one existing category | Nothing structural | The closest is «Επιχειρηματικότητα & Επιδοτήσεις», which fits 5Α (investors) but would mislead for employees (5Γ) and pensioners (5Β). No other category fits all three. | Not recommended |
| B. Add a ninth category «Φορολογία & Φορολογικά Κίνητρα / Tax & Tax Incentives» | Approved list grows to nine | Fits all three exactly; also a natural home for future tax guides | Clean, but changes the approved structure |
| C. Rename category 3 to «Επιχειρηματικότητα, Επιδοτήσεις & Φορολογικά Κίνητρα / Business, Grants & Tax Incentives» | Eight categories kept, one name widened | Fits all three under one heading; keeps the grid at 8 cards | **Recommended** |

**Recommendation: C.** It keeps eight categories, groups the three regimes consistently, and the wider name is accurate
for both business aid and tax incentives. If later there are many tax guides (e.g. de minimis is business aid, not tax),
B can follow.

Until you choose, the current placement stays (5Γ: Εργασία & Εκπαίδευση, 5Β: Επιδόματα & Παροχές, 5Α:
Επιχειρηματικότητα & Επιδοτήσεις). Implementing C is a one-line change to the category name and the three assignments.
