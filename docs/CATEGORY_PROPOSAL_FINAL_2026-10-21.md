# Final category proposal: for approval, NOT implemented (21 Oct 2026)

The site's 11 public categories and all record mappings are unchanged. This proposal builds on the 17 Oct taxonomy proposal and adds the business view.

**Principle.** A category answers *"what do I need?"*. Four other questions are separate filters, never categories:

- who you are;
- your business stage and size;
- your region;
- the kind of content.

## 4. What approval would change

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Display mapping in `src/lib/site/taxonomy.ts` | categories and their mapping |
| Category pages | 8 instead of 11 |
| URLs | old category URLs redirect to the new ones |
| Region filter | added |

What it would not change:

- the eligibility engine;
- record data;
- the database category table, unless you also approve that.

The change is reversible by switching the mapping back.
