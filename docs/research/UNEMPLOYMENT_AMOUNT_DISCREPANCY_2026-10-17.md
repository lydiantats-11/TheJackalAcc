# Unemployment benefit: €565 vs €479 — investigation, 17 Oct 2026

**Finding (strong but not yet officially confirmed):** both ΔΥΠΑ pages describe the **same scheme** (regular unemployment
benefit for salaried workers, «Τακτική επιδότηση ανεργίας»), with the same structure. The English page shows the amounts
that applied from **1 April 2023**; the Greek page shows the amounts consistent with the daily wage in force from
**1 April 2026**. The English page is most likely an outdated translation. The guide stays a **draft**.

## Why we think they are different periods, not different schemes

1. Same scheme name, same three-band structure, same 25-day and +10% rules on both pages.
2. Every figure on the Greek page is the English figure × 1.1794 (565.00 / 479.00; 22.60 / 19.16; band limits
   493.09 / 418.09). The same ratio links the two daily wages: €41.09 / €34.84 = 1.1794.
3. €41.09 is the workers' daily wage from 1/4/2026 (Ministry of Labour notice, 26/3/2026:
   https://ypergasias.gov.gr/nea-afxisi-katotatou-misthou-ena-akomi-simantiko-vima-gia-ti-dievrynsi-tou-diathesimou-eisodimatos-ergazomenon/).
4. €34.84 was the daily wage from 1/4/2023, when the benefit became €479 a month (news report, skai.gr, 1/4/2023 —
   **secondary source**: https://www.skai.gr/news/finance/se-isxy-apo-simera-o-neos-katotatos-misthos-sta-780-eyro).
5. €22.60 is 55% of €41.09; €19.16 is 55% of €34.84 (our calculation, shown as such).

## What is still not confirmed

- Neither ΔΥΠΑ page states an effective date for its table.
- The 2023 daily wage is confirmed here only by a news report; the official minimum-wage decision (ΦΕΚ, 2023) should
  be read to confirm it.
- ΔΥΠΑ has not been asked which page is current.

## How the site should present it (proposal, needs approval)

- Never show €565 and €479 as two valid amounts. Show **€565 (Greek ΔΥΠΑ page)** with a visible note:
  «Η αγγλική σελίδα της ΔΥΠΑ δείχνει παλαιότερα ποσά (479 €), που αντιστοιχούν στο ημερομίσθιο του 2023. Επιβεβαιώστε
  το ποσό στη ΔΥΠΑ.» Until approved, the record keeps «amount not stated».
- Guide stays a draft with this explanation in «Τι δεν γνωρίζουμε ακόμη».
