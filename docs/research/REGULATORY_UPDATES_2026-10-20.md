**72-instalment arrangement, details from Ε.2060/2026.**
- **Which debts.** Overdue by 31/12/2023 and entered with the ΔΟΥ, ΚΕΜΕΦ, ΚΕΒΕΙΣ or customs.
- **Exclusions.** Debts under an arrangement on 21/04/2026, and recovery of EU state aid.
- **Who.** Individuals and legal entities.
- **Conditions.** Income tax returns for the last 5 years filed. No final conviction for tax evasion. Other overdue debts settled or arranged within 1 month.
- **Instalments.** Minimum **€30** a month; first instalment within 3 working days.
- **Where to apply.** myAADE › Ρυθμίσεις Οφειλών › «Αίτηση Ρύθμισης Οφειλών ν. 5313/2026».
- **When it is lost.** Two consecutive instalments unpaid, and other conditions.

**Garnishments after discharge, details from circular 159051/2026.**
- After discharge under art. 192 onward of ν.4738/2020, a garnishment held by a third party is lifted **automatically** from the discharge date.
- Claims arising after discharge go to the debtor; claims from before discharge go to the creditor.
- Secured creditors keep their rights, and claims against co-debtors and guarantors are not affected.
- It applies to natural persons and to the representatives of legal entities.
