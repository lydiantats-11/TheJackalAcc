## 1. De minimis aid — «Τι είναι οι ενισχύσεις ήσσονος σημασίας (de minimis)»

Category: Επιχειρηματικότητα & Επιδοτήσεις · Type: explainer (general, not a programme) · Relevance: Jackal already asks
businesses for "de minimis received in the last 3 years" (profile field `de_minimis_received_3y_eur`).

### Verified (Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2831, read on EUR-Lex 16 Oct 2026)
| Point | Official wording / fact | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Ceiling | «δεν υπερβαίνει το ποσό των 300 000 EUR σε οποιαδήποτε περίοδο 3 ετών» — per single undertaking, per Member State | Art. 3(2) |
| Single undertaking | «Ενιαία επιχείρηση»: all undertakings linked by majority voting rights, power to appoint/remove a board majority, dominant influence by contract or articles, or control of a majority of votes by agreement | Art. 2(2) |
| Not covered | Primary fisheries/aquaculture; processing/marketing of fisheries products tied to price/quantity; primary agricultural production; agricultural processing/marketing tied to price/quantity or passed to producers; export-related aid; aid favouring domestic over imported goods | Art. 1(1) |
| Cumulation | Can be cumulated with SGEI de minimis (Reg. 2023/2832) and, up to the ceiling, with agricultural (1408/2013) and fisheries (717/2014) de minimis | Art. 5 |
| Central register | Member States to record de minimis aid in a central register at the latest from 1 January 2026 | Recital 24 (Art. 6 not yet read) |
| Validity | In force from 1 January 2024 until 31 December 2030 | EUR-Lex metadata |

### Proposed guide structure
1. What de minimis aid is (one paragraph, plain Greek). 2. The €300,000 / 3-year ceiling and what "3 years" means
(Art. 3 — to read in full: rolling period). 3. "Single undertaking" with a simple example. 4. Sectors not covered.
5. Why calls ask for it (many ΕΣΠΑ calls are granted under de minimis — to verify per call). 6. How to find what you
have already received (Greek central register — **to verify**: name, address, how a business consults it).
7. Common mistakes: forgetting linked companies; counting by calendar year. 8. Sources.

### To verify before writing
- Article 3 in full (how the 3-year period is counted) and Article 6 (register obligations).
- The Greek implementation: which authority keeps the de minimis register and how a business checks its own total.
- The previous regulation (EU) 1407/2013 (€200,000) only as history, if useful.

## 2. ΕΣΠΑ — «Πώς διαβάζω και πώς υποβάλλω πρόταση σε πρόσκληση ΕΣΠΑ»

Category: Οδηγοί Βήμα-Βήμα + Επιχειρηματικότητα & Επιδοτήσεις · Type: procedure guide for reading a call, not a
programme page (ΕΣΠΑ is a framework; every call sets its own rules).

### Verified so far
| Point | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Framework | «Συμφωνία Εταιρικής Σχέσης (ΕΣΠΑ) 2021-2027», implemented through operational programmes funded by EU funds incl. the European Regional Development Fund (ΕΤΠΑ) | espa.gr, planning page (last modified 19/01/2021) |
| Calls open separate submission platforms | Example: the General Secretariat for Research & Innovation opened a dedicated platform for an ΕΣΠΑ 2021-2027 research-infrastructure call | gsri.gov.gr announcement |

### Proposed guide structure
1. ΕΣΠΑ is not one programme: calls (προσκλήσεις) define everything. 2. Where official calls are published (**to
verify**: espa.gr calls list, Managing Authorities' sites). 3. How to read a call: beneficiaries, ΚΑΔ, budget and aid
rate, eligible expenses, de minimis or other aid basis, deadline, submission system. 4. Typical prerequisites (**to verify
per call**: TAXISnet credentials, registration on the state-aid information system). 5. Submitting: on the system named
in the call. 6. After submission: evaluation, approval decision, implementation, payment requests (**to verify**).
7. Common mistakes: wrong ΚΑΔ, ineligible expenses, missing the de minimis declaration (**to verify**). 8. Link to
Jackal's open ΕΣΠΑ-related records when they are approved.
